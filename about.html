Six years ago, BLOSBOX began with just four hands and a single briefcase. My father and I started this journey without a single machine, selling our handmade boxes door-to-door, one client at a time. Through tireless dedication and steady year-over-year growth, we have transformed into a state-of-the-art facility where 80% of our production is now automated.

Today, BLOSBOX crafts premium packaging for a wide range of industries — from jewelry, watches, and fashion accessories to perfumes, cosmetics, leather goods, chocolates, wines, drinks, and corporate gifts. With over 1,000,000 boxes delivered to brands and retailers, we continue to blend the soul of our handmade origins with the precision and consistency of modern technology.
