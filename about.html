Six years ago, BLOSBOX began with just four hands and a single briefcase. My father and I started this journey without a single machine, selling our handmade custom rigid boxes door-to-door, one client at a time. Through tireless dedication and steady year-over-year growth, we have transformed into a state-of-the-art packaging manufacturing facility, where 80% of our production is now fully automated for scale and efficiency.

Today, BLOSBOX is a premier manufacturer of luxury custom packaging and premium rigid boxes. We engineer bespoke packaging solutions for a wide range of industries—crafting elegant jewelry and watch boxes, sophisticated cosmetics and perfume packaging, high-end chocolate boxes, and corporate gift packaging.

With over 1,000,000 custom boxes delivered to global brands and retailers, we provide B2B packaging solutions that elevate your brand. We continue to blend the masterful craftsmanship and soul of our handmade origins with the precision, consistency, and scale of modern technology.
