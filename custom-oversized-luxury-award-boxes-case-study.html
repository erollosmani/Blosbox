Engineering Beyond Limits: Custom Oversized Luxury Award Boxes for the Economic Chamber of North Macedonia

November 2026 5 min read Blosbox Atelier

When designing packaging for high-profile institutional recognitions, presence and scale matter immensely. Recently, the Blosbox team tackled an extraordinary structural challenge: engineering a grand-scale, premium presentation box for the Economic Chamber of North Macedonia (Стопанска комора на Северна Македонија) designed to securely house and elevate their prestigious corporate awards and plaques.

The Challenge: Overcoming Standard Paper & Board Size Limits

Standard luxury rigid packaging is typically built around conventional commercial paper sheets and standard printing press formats. For this project, the specifications demanded striking proportions:

  • Large Award Box: Internal dimensions of 420 x 334 mm featuring a deep 330 mm lid and a 30 mm base.
  • Standard Award Box: Internal dimensions of 300 x 220 mm featuring a 240 mm lid and a 20 mm base.

These large-scale dimensions stretch far beyond standard single-sheet paper and board formats. Because producing a seamless rigid box of this magnitude from a single piece of raw material is physically impossible due to paper mill limitations, our atelier engineered an advanced modular construction technique.

Box Variant Internal Dimensions Structural Composition & Engineering Purpose & Application
Large Award Box 420 x 334 mm (330 mm lid / 30 mm base) Multi-part 3mm rigid board core with V-grooved corners and integrated internal collar Housing major institutional plaques with a protective 50mm border
Standard Award Box 300 x 220 mm (240 mm lid / 20 mm base) Modular 3mm structure with precision corner grooving and a 30mm extended neck Precision-tailored for standard recognition awards with 40mm margins

Master Craftsmanship: 3mm Rigid Board, V-Grooving & Velvet Finish

Achieving a high-end luxury look on an oversized format requires meticulous attention to detail and advanced structural techniques:

  • V-Grooved Architectural Precision: Built using robust 3mm thick rigid cardboard, each corner is meticulously V-grooved (milled) prior to wrapping. This technique eliminates bulky, rounded edges, yielding razor-sharp 90-degree corners for a clean, high-end architectural finish.
  • Modular Core Construction: To bypass standard paper sheet limits, the box base was engineered from multiple interlocking structural components, seamlessly integrated with a secondary inner wall element that serves as a functional closing neck (extending 30mm and 25mm respectively).
  • Independent Lid Architecture: Designed with a completely independent, unattached lid and base for effortless handling and smooth unboxing during formal corporate ceremonies.
  • Tactile Luxury & Plotter-Cut Details: Draped entirely in rich black velvet, the exterior features razor-sharp branding crafted using a cutting plotter to precisely cut the custom chrome graphics, followed by a professional heat press to permanently bond the metallic element onto the textured velvet surface.

Elevate Your Brand with Custom Rigid Packaging

This project for the Economic Chamber of North Macedonia demonstrates what is possible when bespoke engineering meets uncompromising luxury packaging design. By looking past manufacturing limitations and mastering large-format V-grooving and precision detailing, Blosbox turns unique structural challenges into unforgettable tactile masterpieces.
