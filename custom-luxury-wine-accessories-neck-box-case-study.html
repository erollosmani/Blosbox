Contour-Cut Precision: Custom Neck-Style Luxury Rigid Box for a Sommelier Wine Accessories Set

November 2026 4 min read Blosbox Atelier

When presenting fine metal sommelier instruments—stainless-steel waiter's corkscrews, conical stoppers, drip collars, and pourer spouts—generic rectangular trays never match the prestige of the hardware inside. For a European wine and gifting client, Blosbox engineered a custom neck-style rigid presentation box in red and black velvet where every curved metal contour locks cleanly into a smooth, fabric-laminated die-cut sponge insert.

The Challenge: Cradling Irregular Metal Curves with Zero Movement

Unlike symmetrical bottles or flat gift cards, a four-piece sommelier set combines completely different geometries, weights, and tapered silhouettes in a single shallow tray:

  • Complex Contours & Heavy Hardware: A hinged sommelier knife, a circular drip collar, a tapered conical stopper, and an angled pourer spout require exact negative-space tracing so no piece shifts or rattles during European transport.
  • Elevating Raw Protective Sponge: Unlaminated open-cell foam looks utilitarian in high-end spirits and wine gifting—the interior deck needed a smooth, textile-finished surface without losing compression grip.
  • Two-Tone Tactile Prestige: Using 1.2mm rigid greyboard, the structure needed a crisp flush-closing neck architecture pairing a rich red velvet lid with a deep black velvet base.
Component Material & Finish Structural & Tooling Detail
Outer Rigid Structure 1.2mm Rigid Greyboard Neck-style (shoulder) two-piece construction with independent red lid and black inner-collared base
Exterior Wraps Red & Black Velvet Paper Luxurious plush velvet lid contrasted with deep matte-black velvet body and closing neck
Custom Interior Insert Smooth Fabric-Laminated Sponge High-density protective sponge pre-laminated in black textile, precision contour die-cut to all 4 tools

Master Craftsmanship: Contour Die-Cutting & Fabric-Laminated Sponge

Upon delivery, the client was genuinely amazed by how accurately every accessory nestled into the insert. Achieving that effortless "fit like a glove" tolerance came down to three manufacturing steps:

  • Smooth Textile Lamination Before Cutting: Rather than lining cavities after cutting, Blosbox first laminated a fine, smooth black fabric directly onto high-density sponge sheets. When die-cut, the top deck stays clean, wrinkle-free, and silky to the touch.
  • Custom Contour Die-Lines: Each wine tool was physically measured and digitized so bespoke cutting dies traced every curve of the corkscrew handle, bottle stopper taper, ring circumference, and pourer profile with razor-sharp edge definition.
  • Flush Neck-Style 1.2mm Greyboard Reveal: Wrapped in tactile Red and Black velvet over sturdy 1.2mm rigid greyboard, the recessed black shoulder creates a seamless visual transition between lid lift and hardware display.
